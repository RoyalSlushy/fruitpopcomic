About

Fruit Pop Comic

A webcomic, and the hub for everything around it — the pages themselves, the cast, and the world they live in, all in one place instead of scattered across platforms.

What's real here, and what isn't

Everything on this site is the creator's own work. The logo and the character art are finished. The comic pages are rough drafts — working pencils in a single colour, shown to establish page format and panel grammar, not final art. The wiki is genuinely empty.

Nothing has been invented to fill space: no page counts, no release dates, no reader numbers, no character names beyond the one the drafts actually give.

Reading it

Pages are 1080 × 1620 — a 2:3 portrait that fills a phone screen almost exactly. Use the arrows, the filmstrip, or the ← and → keys.

A note on access

Dialogue is hand-lettered into the artwork, so it can't be selected, searched, translated, or read by a screen reader. Transcripts would fix that and would have to be written by hand. It's an open decision, flagged rather than quietly skipped.
